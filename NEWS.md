# BGS 0.1.0

- `Bedrock()`, `Superficial()` and `Seabed()` report the geology mapped by the
  British Geological Survey at points given by latitude and longitude,
  British National Grid coordinates or Ordnance Survey grid references.
- `LatLongToBNG()`, `BNGToLatLong()` and `GridRefToBNG()` convert between
  these coordinates.
- `BGSSources()` lists the maps' versions and licence.
