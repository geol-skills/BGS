# BGS 0.1.0

- `Bedrock()` and `Superficial()` report the geology mapped by the British
  Geological Survey, on land or at sea, at points given by latitude and
  longitude, British National Grid coordinates or Ordnance Survey grid
  references.
- `LatLongToBNG()`, `BNGToLatLong()` and `GridRefToBNG()` convert between
  these coordinates.
- `BGSSources()` lists the maps' versions and licence.
